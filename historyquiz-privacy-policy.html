Privacy Policy

みんなで日本史対戦

Effective Date: September 12, 2026

HINATA NAKAMURA ("Service Provider", "we", or "us") operates the mobile application みんなで日本史対戦 ("Application"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Application.

1. Information You Provide

The Application does not ask you to enter your name, postal address, telephone number, email address, or a password to play. However, it uses third-party services that process identifiers and technical information as described below.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this information to respond to your inquiry and handle support or privacy requests.

2. Anonymous Authentication and Online Multiplayer

The Application uses Unity Gaming Services, including Unity Authentication and Unity Relay, to authenticate players and support online matches.

Unity Authentication can automatically create an anonymous player account and assign a player ID without asking you to register. Authentication credentials may be cached on your device and reused on later launches. Anonymous authentication does not mean that no data is collected, and its records are not necessarily deleted when a match ends. Service initialization and authentication may occur when the Application starts, before you choose online play.

For online matches, Unity processes connection and session information, such as IP addresses, player identifiers, authentication information, and join codes. Gameplay information needed to run the match, including answer actions, scores, and match results, is exchanged between the participating devices through the networking service. The other player can see the game information displayed during the match and in its results.

3. Advertising

The Application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Depending on the platform, SDK configuration, permissions, and applicable consent choices, Google may process:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location;
  • Device information and advertising or other device identifiers;
  • Advertising activity and app interactions, such as ad views and taps;
  • Crash logs, diagnostic information, and performance data.

This information is used for advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, service improvement, and fraud prevention. On iOS, access to the advertising identifier is subject to your App Tracking Transparency permission. You can review tracking permissions in your device settings. Denying tracking permission does not stop all technical data processing or necessarily remove advertisements.
